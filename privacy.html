Your information

Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This notice explains how the Kabeya Ilunga website uses optional analytics and handles information you choose to send to the studio.

Essential storage

The site stores your analytics choice in your browser so it can remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics. This preference is kept in local browser storage and is not used to identify you.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics 4 is optional. It is not loaded unless you choose “Accept analytics”. If accepted, Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies to measure site visits. Advertising storage and advertising personalisation remain disabled.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using “Privacy and cookies” in the footer. Choosing “Reject optional” keeps Google Analytics disabled.

Contact enquiries

If you send a message through the contact form, the details you provide, such as your name, email address, subject and message, are processed by FormSubmit and forwarded to the studio at studio@kabeyailunga.com. Do not include sensitive personal information. FormSubmit handles submissions under its own privacy terms and retention practices.

Embedded exhibition

The Solo Show page embeds content hosted by Artsteps. When you view it, Artsteps and its providers may process technical data or use cookies under their own privacy and cookie notices. The exhibition also offers a link to open the experience directly on its host.

Your choices and contact

You can reject optional analytics or update your choice from the footer at any time. For questions about information sent directly to the studio, email studio@kabeyailunga.com.
